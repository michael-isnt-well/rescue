Pawprints to Freedom is a UK-registered charity (charity no. 1194754) that began in 2017 and now runs on a network of more than 100 volunteers. It rescues dogs in Romania — where it runs its own shelter — and rehomes them across mainland UK. Some dogs are already in UK foster homes or kennels by the time they're listed. This is the rescue we adopted Barnie through.

## The fee and what it covers

The standard adoption fee for mainland UK is **around £520**, though it can vary depending on the individual dog and your location. It covers **transport to the UK, vaccinations, microchip, pet passport, and spay/neuter** — so the figure is close to the true all-in cost, not a starting point.

**Fostering is free.** You can foster a dog (with the option to adopt later) at no cost, which is a good route in if you're not ready to commit to the full fee up front.

## The process

A **virtual home check is required** before you can adopt, along with a **£20 refundable deposit** that's deducted from the adoption fee. Applications start through their website.

Their rehoming decisions are made per dog, based on each dog's temperament and assessment:

- **Children** — generally homes with children aged 4+, with the exact minimum age depending on the individual dog.
- **Cats** — considered case by case; dogs are cat-tested in Romania or in UK foster homes where possible.
- **No garden** — flat and gardenless applications are considered depending on the dog's energy, temperament, and access to nearby green space.

## Transport

Dogs travel from Romania on the rescue's own dedicated transport (the "Happy Bus"), which runs roughly every few weeks as vehicle capacity allows. In practice, expect somewhere around **4 to 8 weeks from reservation to arrival**.

::callout{type="info"}
Transport timings are approximate and rehoming decisions are made dog by dog, so treat the timeline and the policies above as a guide and confirm the specifics for your chosen dog when you apply.
::

## After you adopt

Adopters get **lifetime rescue back-up** (the rescue will always take a dog back if things don't work out), access to dedicated **post-adoption Facebook groups**, and ongoing advice from the rescue's volunteers.
