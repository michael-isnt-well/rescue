Paws2Rescue is a registered charity in England and Wales (no. 1156882, registered in 2014) that rehomes dogs from Romania to the UK. It describes itself as "first and foremost" a charity aimed at reducing the suffering of dogs in Romania.

## The fee and what it covers

From 1 January 2026, the adoption fee for a dog or puppy from overseas is **£525**, or **£425 for dogs over 8**. Dogs already in the UK cost £250, unless they came from overseas and are in foster, in which case the overseas fees apply.

The fee covers multiple blood tests (including babesia, leishmania, giardia, brucellosis and a heartworm panel), vaccinations, microchip, passport and health book, vet checks, administration, and **transport from Romania to the UK**. The rescue notes that its actual costs exceed the fee.

The dog's own neutering is **not** included. For every dog adopted, the rescue funds the neutering of another dog in Romania.

## Who they rehome to

- **Garden:** a fully secure garden is required, with no gaps a dog could escape through and fencing of at least 6ft (5.5ft for smaller dogs). Ground-floor flats with secure outdoor access are considered, with a more limited choice of dogs.
- **Children:** children in the household should ideally be over 8, or over 12 for dogs from public shelters. Visiting children need to be mentioned in the application.
- **Cats:** cat-tested dogs are available, though the rescue is clear that outcomes can't be guaranteed.

## Timescales

The rescue says the vaccinations and passport usually take around a month to complete.

## After you adopt

The rescue says it keeps in contact with adopters and is there to support them after adoption.
