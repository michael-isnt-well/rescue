All Dogs Great and Small Rescue rehomes abandoned dogs from Romania, providing medical care and fostering them until they find homes in the UK.

## The fee and what it covers

The rescue charges an **adoption donation of £250** per dog, which covers spaying or neutering (for dogs over 7 months), a vet check, EU pet passport, microchip, vaccinations (DHPPi, leptospirosis and rabies), flea and worm treatment, and 4DX and Brucellosis tests.

**Transport is quoted separately**, at about **£255** from Romania to your home address in England and Wales, or to a meeting point (negotiable, in the Fife area) in Scotland. That makes the all-in cost roughly £505.

## The process

As part of its checks, the rescue asks applicants for a reference from a vet or another professional.

- **Cats:** at the rescue's discretion. It says most of its dogs are fine with cats, but there isn't always a way to test this.
- **Children:** the rescue advises using your own discretion if you have young children.

## After you adopt

The rescue offers **rescue back-up for 3 months** after adoption, and says it will take the dog back.

We couldn't find a charity number on the rescue's website, and we found no matching entry on the Charity Commission's register for England and Wales (checked 9 October 2026). Not every rescue group is a registered charity, so ask the rescue directly about its status if it matters to you.
