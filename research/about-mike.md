# About Mike: brief for the side-business scout

Read this before each run. It overrides the summary in the routine prompt. It isn't
published: it lives only on the `research/app-scout` branch. Last updated: 2026-10-15.

## Skills (from his day job and side projects)

- **Mobile:** currently manages an Android and iOS app in a corporate setting, covering
  releases, store listings and keeping it working across OS updates.
- **Front-end web:** builds and maintains websites. Side projects use Nuxt/Vue static sites
  and interactive tools (see rescuejourney.co.uk).
- **Back end:** .NET APIs. Uses a document database occasionally.
- **Quality:** uses automated test tooling; tests and CI are part of how he works.
- **Data and content:** has built a directory from verified UK open data (the Charity
  Commission register) and writes sourced, SEO-led content.
- **Ways of working:** develops quickly with AI coding tools. Experienced in corporate
  process, so he can write a spec, manage releases and deal with stakeholders. He would
  like *less* of the process and meetings.

## Goal and time

- **Now:** part-time, in evenings and weekends, alongside a full-time corporate job.
- **Long term:** this could become his ONLY work, at **25–30 hours a week**. So favour
  ideas that can grow into recurring, sustainable income (retainers, subscriptions,
  repeatable productised services, a product with recurring revenue) over one-off gigs
  that can't scale past a hobby.
- Honesty first: no hype, no dark patterns, no invented demand.

## What this opens up (angles to test, not conclusions)

- **Native mobile is in scope** for products. A web-first version is still preferred
  when it's enough, but don't rule out a native app where it's clearly the better tool.
- **App care for SMEs:** small businesses with an iOS or Android app built by an agency
  or a freelancer who has since left, now facing store policy deadlines, OS-update
  breakage or SDK deprecations. Check for evidence of demand and real budgets.
- **API and integration work:** .NET or other APIs connecting tools SMEs already use,
  and small, well-documented data APIs, especially built from UK open data.
- **Quality as a service:** automated testing or release set-up for small software teams
  that have none, if evidence shows they will pay for it.

## Constraints

- Nothing that conflicts with his current employer, such as competing products, the same
  clients, or work that uses his employer's IP. If an idea sits close to his employer's
  sector, flag it.
- While he's employed: no on-call support or response-time promises; asynchronous,
  fixed-scope work is best.
- No regulated advice (tax, legal, financial, medical or veterinary).
